Two alarms. One for getting ready, one for going.

Pick how early you want the heads-up. The start alarm covers the lock screen and rings even in silent mode.

Pre-reminder
4:45 PM

A gentle nudge 15 minutes before. Lead time from 5 minutes to a day.

Start alarm
5:00 PM

Full screen and loud. Start, snooze or mark done right from the lock screen.

Everything for the task, in one place

No more digging through chats for the address or the phone number.

Quick entry

Type a name, tap a time, save. You see "Alarms set: 4:45 PM and 5:00 PM".

Notes and checklist

Description with links, a checklist, and files or photos from your phone or camera.

People

Add who's involved, with Call, SMS, WhatsApp and Email buttons one tap away.

Place

Paste a Google Maps link or an address and tap Navigate when it's time to leave.

Your way

Categories, priority, repeat, sound, vibration and snooze per task. High and Urgent keep ringing until you answer.

Reliable

Alarms survive restarts, updates and time-zone changes. Automatic backups and in-app updates keep you current.

See it in action

Clean, calm and built for one-handed use. Light and dark themes.

Your tasks stay on your phone

Your tasks are never sent anywhere. The only internet use is a daily check for app updates, which you can turn off. Back up to one .tbk file in a folder you pick, automatically every day, week or month.

  • Tasks never leave your phone
  • No account, no ads, no analytics
  • Android cloud backup turned off
  • Backups checked before they're restored
  • Update checks can be turned off

Install in a minute

TaskBell is installed from an APK file, not the Play Store.

  1. Download
    On your Android phone, tap Download APK.
  2. Open the file
    If asked, allow your browser or file manager to install apps.
  3. Set up
    Follow the three setup steps, then ring the test alarm.
Xiaomi, Oppo, Realme, Vivo, Samsung or Huawei? Open Settings > Alarm health in TaskBell. It shows the steps for your phone so alarms ring on time.
